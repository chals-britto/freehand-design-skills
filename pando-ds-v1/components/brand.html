Usage

Figma has no usage notes for these two sets. The only text in the "Pando - Pi branding" section is the Core Colors guideline, quoted as written.

Core Colors (Figma frame P46, 7902:1192)

The primary palette uses a combination of bold natural hues: A Yellow named “Quaking aspen trees” and “Forrest” for Green and Charcoal represents as Earth

The cream shade is a lightened tint of our yellow, while the forest color is a darkened tone. A lighter cream and toned white add contrast and flexibility for accessibility, and they work together to emphasize the Pando yellow.

Pando Yellow#FFE01B · R255 G224 B0 · C62 M22 Y100 K0
Pando Green#003D31 · R255 G255 B255 · C0 M0 Y0 K0
Charcoal#0F0F0F · R15 G188 B115 · C75 M0 Y75 K0

The RGB and CMYK values above are copied from Figma and do not match the hex values. See Open issues.

Accessibility

Alt text

Logo: alt="Pando". Pi mark: an alt that names it (e.g. alt="Pi"), or alt="" when a visible label sits next to it.

Added in review — not in Figma.

Pick the variant for the ground

Flat White and Gradient White disappear on white. Flat Green on Charcoal is very low contrast. The gallery shows every variant on both grounds.

Open issues

  • Core Colors text contradicts itself: Pando Yellow #FFE01B is listed as R255 G224 B0 (= #FFE000); Pando Green #003D31 as R255 G255 B255 / C0 M0 Y0 K0 (white); Charcoal #0F0F0F as R15 G188 B115 / C75 M0 Y75 K0 (a green).
  • Two yellows: the dot in Flat Black and Flat White is #FFDD33; every other yellow is #FFE01B.
  • Pi-Avatar has 6 of 8 combinations: Ai Stars=Yes exists only with Shadow=Yes.
  • Flat Black and Flat White are 106.7 tall; the other logos are 106.
  • Shadow variants: the SVG file is larger than the component (shadow included). Align by the shape, not the file box.
  • No clear space, minimum size or placement rules in Figma. One Pando-Logo instance on the page is used at 74 × 21 (8388:1072).
  • Brand colours (#FFE01B, #003D31, #0F0F0F, #F2C200, #211203, #FFEC75, #F8CF0C, #FFDE72, #FFDD33) are not in tokens.css.
  • Colour frame P48 (7902:1213: Functional / Light / Accent, 15 swatches) has no names or values in text; not documented.
