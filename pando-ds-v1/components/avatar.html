Usage

Placement & convention

Displayed within lists, cards or on the menu.

Which avatar

  • Icon Avatar — an entity, shown by its indicator icon.
  • Text Avatar — a user, shown by initials.
  • Comment Avatar — who wrote a comment: T Transporter, S Shipper.

States

  • Default — Status Light fill.
  • Active Card — white fill inside an active card (Dark: #343440).
  • Selected — white check on Pando blue.
  • Checkbox — same look as Active Card in Figma.

Accessibility

Text alternative

If the avatar carries meaning, give it role="img" and an aria-label (“Factory”, “Transporter”). If a visible name sits next to it, mark it aria-hidden="true". Added in review — not in Figma.

Contrast

Initials #0076DD on white: 4.53 : 1. Comment Avatar Dark · Transporter: 2.97 : 1 — below 4.5 : 1. Kept as Figma for now.
