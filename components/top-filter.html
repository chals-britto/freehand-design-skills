Top Filter

The full-width band above a list: the source → destination read-out, More Filters, the metric Widget Groups with their view actions, and the date on the right. Built entirely from the kit’s Metrics and Filter By pieces; only the background, the layout and .Filter-Actions are new.

Try it

Click a metric to make it Active (click again to clear). Open More Filters (funnel) and pick a value: the pill turns Filled. Press the middle .Filter-Actions button (Reset View) to clear both. The log shows the pds-top-filter-change state. Option lists are the kit Filter Dropdown’s Figma samples.

Show Action Button is not built — its button text could not be read from Figma (see Open issues).

Variants

All 5 Types at full width, with Figma’s sample content and instance overrides (Pending Active, On Hold red number, brown / purple / blue highlight lines, green numbers on the right group). Booleans at their defaults. Then .Filter-Actions on its own.

Usage

Types

  • Default — source → destination, More Filters, three Widget Groups, date and a right Widget Group.
  • Filter at bottom — widgets first, the open More Filters under them.
  • Only stats — widgets and date, no filters.
  • No Stats — one row: source, open More Filters, date.
  • Swappable — one row with a slot for product content, More Filters, date.

Booleans

  • Show Filters (default on) — More Filters.
  • Show Date Selection Filter (default on).
  • Show Additional Time (default off) — “Bid Ends 0:00:00”, the kit .Date and Time Type=Time.
  • Show Action Button (default off) — not built, see Open issues.

Not in Figma

Figma has no usage text for Top Filter. The wiring here — metric click toggles Active, filters fill through the kit, Reset View clears both, any change reports the applied state — is the build’s reading and needs confirming.

Accessibility

Composed controls keep their roles

Metrics are kit <button aria-pressed> tiles; pills, More Filters and the date trigger are the kit Filter By buttons. Each Widget Group is a role="group" named “Widget Type”.

.Filter-Actions

Three icon buttons in a group named “View actions”, labelled from the Figma frame names: “All Widgets”, “Reset View”, “Add View”. Focus ring 2px --pando-blue, offset 2 — added in review, not in Figma.

Read-outs

The movement mark is role="img" named “to”, so the source reads “<Source> to All Consignees”. The right group is No Click metrics (text only, no buttons).

Open issues

  • Show Action Button: its Text Button (with an Indicator Icon/Details 14) is hidden in every variant and its label text could not be read. Not built. The Swappable variant also hides a Primary Button whose text could not be read.
  • Behaviour is not written in Figma: single vs multiple Active metrics, what All Widgets / Add View / Reset View do, whether metric clicks and filters combine. Built as described under Usage.
  • Filter at bottom: the open More Filters has no close ×, so it can’t be collapsed. Kept as Figma.
  • Off-token colours: background stops #3F6ABF / #18283B (gradient angle differs from --gradient-ink-blue), source text #18283B, movement line #36577E, slot text #000000; number gradients reuse the stops of --gradient-rejection-red / --gradient-forest-green at the kit metric angle.
  • Kit metric label is one line with ellipsis; Figma wraps “Total Weight (LBS)” onto two lines. Overridden inside .pds-top-filter (label bottom-aligned in a 20px box).
  • Atom variant missing in kit: the Swappable slot’s “refresh-line” icon is not in the icon set — the slot renders without it. The slot is a placeholder (.Swappable Content), not a component.
  • Line heights: “All Consignees” 14 / 10 and “<Source>” 14 / 18 have no type token.
  • Background blur 100 on the white veil is not built (it only blurs the gradient under it, no visible effect). Inner shadow is not one of --shadow-1..4.
  • Small offsets: No Stats left row sits at y 11 (Figma 10) inside the 46 band; Only stats date at y 15 (Figma 14). .Filter-Actions dividers are drawn inside (Figma 0.5 centre stroke).
  • No responsive behaviour in Figma (1280 only). The band does not wrap; the doc page draws it at Figma’s 1280 and scrolls sideways when narrower.
  • Hidden layers not built: Movement Indicator (Status/Movement Type), the 4th and 5th Widget Groups, the second right Widget Group.
  • Contrast (deferred): number gradients are light at the top — red #E96262 end ≈ 3.0 : 1 and green #19A476 end ≈ 2.9 : 1 on the metric tile (dark ends pass); on the right group (no tile fill) ≈ 2.3 : 1 against the band. Active label white on #3F6ABF 5.2 : 1 passes.
