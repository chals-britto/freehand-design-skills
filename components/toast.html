Toast

Toast is a lightweight and easily customizable Alert message that display a status update, reflecting a user or system action. Toasts are meant to be short and brief.

Usage

Placement & convention

Placed below the main header and center of the screen. The toast sits directly under the header with no gap.

When not to use

  • For critical information, or anything the person must act on to continue. Use a dialog, a field error or an inline message instead.
  • As the only place a message exists. If it matters later, it must also be shown somewhere else (a list, a status, a log).

Sources: Fluent 2 Toast · WCAG 2.2.1 · Carbon Notification

Content

  • Title: one short line.
  • Body: at most 2 lines. Longer text is cut off.
  • At most one action link, with a label of 1–2 words.

Sources: Carbon Notification · Fluent 2 Toast

Closing

  • Persistent = No: closes by itself after 4 seconds.
  • While the pointer is over the toast (or keyboard focus is inside it), it stays. The 4 seconds restart when the pointer leaves.
  • Persistent = Yes: stays until the close × is pressed.

Action link

  • Optional, at most one.
  • Navigation only (“take me there”), e.g. View. V1 has no undo.

Accessibility

Announced without moving focus

Success and Info use role="status" (read politely). Error and Warning use role="alert" (read immediately). toast.js sets this for you.

Source: WCAG 2.2 · 4.1.3 Status messages

Close and action are buttons

The × has the label “Close”. Both the × and the action link can be reached with Tab and pressed with Enter or Space.

Open for the accessibility pass

White text on Success (4.2 : 1) and Error (3.6 : 1) is below 4.5 : 1. Kept as Figma for now.
