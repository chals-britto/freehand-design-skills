Usage

Pick the family, then the size

  • Indicator icons come in 14 and 20. Use 14 inside rows, chips and inputs; 20 on cards and section headers.
  • Action icons (10px line) sit inside small action buttons and table row actions.
  • Status icons (14 solid) mark a shipment’s indent type, movement type, tracking method and “other” flags.
  • Menu icons (20, two-colour) are for the side navigation only.

From the Figma frame names on the Icons page.

Colour

Single-colour icons are drawn with currentColor, so they take the text colour of their parent. Set color on the wrapper, or use .pds-icon--indicator, --action or --status for the Figma colours. Multi-colour icons (menu, file types, logos, map, toast states) keep their own colours.

Inline, not <img>

currentColor only works when the SVG is inline. In an <img> a single-colour icon renders black. Copy the inline SVG from the gallery, or load the file and insert it.

Accessibility

Decorative next to text

When an icon sits next to a visible label, hide it: aria-hidden="true" on the wrapper (and focusable="false" on the SVG).

Meaningful on its own

Icon-only buttons need a name: <button aria-label="Download">. A status icon that carries meaning (Hazardous, High priority) needs text nearby or role="img" aria-label="…" on the SVG.
