Loaders-Gif

Accessibility

Decorative image

Use alt="". Announce loading on the region instead: aria-busy="true" on the area being loaded, and a text label in a role="status" element. Figma has no label copy.

Motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the <picture> serves the still PNG. Added in review — not in Figma.

Pause, stop, hide

The loops have no pause control. Content moving for more than 5 s needs one (WCAG 2.2.2) unless the loader is gone sooner.

Source: WCAG 2.2.2

Open issues

  • All four are raster GIFs (illustration / photographic art), not rebuildable in CSS or SVG. Shipped as the original files.
  • Upload-Data-Animation is 5.7 MB — very heavy for a loader.
  • Box and Upload are marked “Temporary design” in Figma.
  • No loading text or label for any of them; no sizes; no spinner for inline or button loading.
  • Loaders-Gif has no Figma description and uses photographic art, unlike the flat illustrations of the other three.
  • Only Upload has a 2× asset. Loaders-Gif and Document are 1×; Box is 250 × 189 shown at 0.8×.
  • Reduced-motion stills are hand-picked frames (Loaders-Gif 74, Box 3, Upload 173, Document 3); frame 0 of Loaders-Gif is nearly empty.
  • No pause or stop control for the loops (WCAG 2.2.2).
  • Not adopted: the loose “Animation” frame 4636:2474 next to the loaders (“Tracking Shipment” map) is not a component.

Industry gaps — not added

  • Spinner in two sizes: large for page or section (with overlay), small inline beside a button. Carbon Loading
  • Optional short status text under the indicator, an -ing phrase of three words or less. Carbon Loading · Fluent 2 Spinner
  • Label linked with aria-labelledby when the wait is over 3 s. Fluent 2 Spinner
  • Determinate progress bar when progress is known. Fluent 2 Spinner
