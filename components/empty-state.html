Usage

Uses

  • When there is no data provided, display for friendly tips.
  • User tutorial to create something in fresh new situation.
  • Empty states are often a chance to celebrate, educate, and inform people of what they can do next.

Source: Figma .documentation (2407:8045)

Placement & convention

Center of the section, with descriptions that add useful and relevant additional information.

Source: Figma .documentation (2407:8045)

Three kinds

  • Information-focused — tells the user the container is empty, rather than letting them assume the system is broken. Can carry onboarding content and other learning opportunities.
  • Action-focused — urges users toward actions that will fill up the space.
  • Celebration-focused — most rare. The empty state is a good thing and becomes success feedback.

Source: Figma page note (4545:3587)

Accessibility

Alt text

Shown alone, the picture is the only content: set alt to the set’s hiddenText from data.js (Figma’s own hidden copy). With visible text beside it, use alt="".

Text inside pictures

No Bids Found, No Deliveries, No Master Data and No SKU (Dark) draw text into the image. Assistive tech cannot read it and it does not scale. Its contrast is 4.24 : 1 on Light and 2.99 : 1 on Dark — both below 4.5 : 1. Kept as Figma for now.

Open issues

  • No title, body or action in Figma. The documentation says “with descriptions”, but every variant’s “Empty State Text” layer is hidden and has no text style. Needs a decision before text is added.
  • Illustrations are raster image fills, not vectors, so SVG export only wraps a multi-MB bitmap. PNGs here are cut at 1× from a Figma page render and look soft on 2× screens. Export 2× per variant for production.
  • Transparent corners were made by removing the #FAFAFA canvas colour; a faint light edge may show on dark surfaces.
  • Dark backdrop #282833 is not in tokens.css.
  • Hidden copy is inconsistent (“No SKu”, “No SEarch”, “no CLIENTS”, “NO PERMISSION”). “Search” and “Search Results” share the hidden text “No SEarch”.
  • Baked-in text contrast below 4.5 : 1 (4.24 : 1 Light, 2.99 : 1 Dark).

Industry gaps — not added

  • An action under the copy: primary button, or tertiary when several empty states show at once; or a link. Carbon
